# Brag Plan: Yozigo Cinemas — Next-Gen Movie Booking System

## 1. Project Planning Rubric

- **What is it?** A full-stack, real-time cinema ticket booking platform featuring dynamic showtimes, interactive seat matrix selection, food & beverage combos, and instant digital QR ticketing.
- **Who is it for?** Movie lovers seeking an ultra-smooth, premium booking experience without ticket queues or clunky interfaces.
- **What sets it apart?** Blazing fast response times, immersive dark-mode cinema aesthetic, real-time tiered seat visualization (VIP/Premium/Standard), and end-to-end FastAPI + React architecture.
- **Most impressive claim:** "Zero-latency interactive seat booking with instant QR-coded digital tickets."
- **Visual hook:** Glowing neon cinema entrance revealing curated blockbusters (*Leo, Fight Club, The Dark Knight Rises*).
- **Tone:** `cinematic` + `polished` — bold typography, deep navy/obsidian background, rose-red & amber gold glows, high-energy rhythm.
- **Target Duration:** 26 seconds (780 frames @ 30fps).
- **Target Resolution:** 1920 × 1080 (Landscape 16:9), 30 fps.
